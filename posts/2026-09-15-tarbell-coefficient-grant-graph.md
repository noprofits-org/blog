The contribution is rematchability, not a verdict. Against Coefficient Giving’s
grants-index snapshot dated 2026-09-11, Tarbell’s own
[About](https://www.tarbellcenter.org/about) and
[Ethics](https://www.tarbellcenter.org/ethics-and-standards) pages, Survival
and Flourishing Fund recommendation lists, and
[Good Ventures Foundation](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/461008520)
Forms 990-PF through FYE June 2025, Kevin Bass’s Tarbell-named Coefficient
total rematches at **$5,291,930** with no KILLs. Motive is parked. What
rematched is below. What stays **OPEN** is F3: the public-filing vehicle for
any donated Moskovitz Anthropic stake is still unidentified.

The rows under audit are the Tarbell package in
[Bass’s metr-money-figure repository](https://github.com/kevinnbass/metr-money-figure)
— a claim package, not a source of amounts.

## Frozen claims

These sentences are frozen as of 2026-09-15. A kill is a primary document that
contradicts the sentence.

**F1 — Tarbell ← Coefficient**

> As of 2026-09-15, Coefficient Giving’s public grants index (snapshot 2026-09-11) lists three awards naming “Tarbell Center for AI Journalism” totaling $5,291,930 ($816,000 General Support 2024-11-17; $2,888,000 Operating Costs 2025-03-06; $1,587,930 General Support 2025-07-15), and Tarbell’s own About page lists Coefficient Giving as a $1M+ supporter for 2023–2026; Tarbell’s Ethics page states that as of 2025 the majority of its funding originates with Coefficient Giving.

**F2 — No direct GVF / Coefficient → METR (through checked filings)**

> As of the Coefficient grants-index snapshot dated 2026-09-11 and Good Ventures Foundation Forms 990-PF through FYE June 2025, no grant row names “METR” or “Model Evaluation and Threat Research” as grantee (Bass “Direct: none found”; Quill rematch of the same index found zero METR-named awards).

**F3 — Anthropic-share vehicle unidentified on public filings (OPEN)**

> As of GVF Forms 990-PF through FYE June 2025, no investment schedule line names Anthropic; Bass’s $7.7B figure is an upper bound (Forbes “less than 0.8%” × Series H post-money), not a marked GVF holding; the legal vehicle holding any donated Moskovitz Anthropic stake remains unidentified on public filings (open candidates: post-June-2025 GVF FY2026 return; SVCF/NPT donor-advised accounts).

F3 is **OPEN**. Nothing in this rematch identifies the holder.

## Gaps / next closes

These are residual holes, not KILLs.

**Live Coefficient grant pages.** The rematch checked the Bass slugs for
TB01–TB04 — including
[training-for-good-operating-costs-and-tarbell-fellowship](https://coefficientgiving.org/grants/training-for-good-operating-costs-and-tarbell-fellowship/),
[general-support-3](https://coefficientgiving.org/grants/general-support-3/),
[general-support-49](https://coefficientgiving.org/grants/general-support-49/),
and
[operating-costs](https://coefficientgiving.org/grants/operating-costs/).
All four returned 404. The Wayback availability API reported no
`archived_snapshots` for those URLs. An older Open Philanthropy slug for
Tarbell general support likewise had no snapshot and now redirects to a
Coefficient focus-area hub, not a grant page.

**GVF cash trail is incomplete for the larger 2025 awards.** The FY2023 990-PF
in the intake shows a Players Philanthropy Fund line of $287,750 with purpose
text naming a Tarbell fellow extension — a partial cash trail for the TB01
neighborhood, not the $999,000 award-date figure. FY2024 has a PPF $816,000
“GENERAL SUPPORT” line (same amount as TB02; purpose text does not name
Tarbell — do not treat it as a definitive TB02 disbursement). No matching
$2,888,000 paid line for TB04 appears on the FY2024 schedule in this intake.
TB03’s award date is 2025-07-15, after FYE 2025-06-30, so it would not appear
on the returns already checked.

**GVF FY2026.** The next Good Ventures Foundation 990-PF (period beginning
after June 2025) is the first filing that could show TB03 cash and any
post-June-2025 investment-schedule change. Until that return is public, F3
stays OPEN.

**NPT / SVCF.** National Philanthropic Trust and Silicon Valley Community
Foundation donor-advised accounts remain open candidates for a donated
Anthropic stake. They are not identified as holders on the filings reviewed
through FY2025.

**SFF recommendations are not paid-grant lines.** TB05 and TB06 rematch as
published recommendations. Confirmation of cash paid, and of the TB06 match,
is still OPEN.
