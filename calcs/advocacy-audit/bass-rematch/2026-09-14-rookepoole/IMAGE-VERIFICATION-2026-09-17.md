## Method notes

- Inspection by region crops of the 1657×4096 original at native resolution;
  three bands (y 480–1180, 1150–1950, 1950–2750, 2700–3500) read directly.
- Amounts above were read from edge labels, not node boxes; pairing
  label→node is by adjacency and is provisional for labels not directly
  touching a node border.
- Anything not listed here remains OPEN; this file upgrades the
  transcription from "unverifiable hearsay" to "visually confirmed as drawn"
  for the listed rows — it does NOT upgrade any dollar to a filed amount.
  The rematch standard is unchanged: a graphic label is a claim, and only a
  primary document promotes a claim to KEEP.
